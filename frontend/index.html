🔒 Sealed Fortune

Guess the hidden number. Nobody can see it - not the host, not other players, not anyone reading the chain - until reveal.

On a transparent chain this game is impossible. With Zama FHEVM, the winning number and every guess stay encrypted while the game is played.

Demo mode needs no wallet. Sepolia mode needs MetaMask + a deployed contract.

How it works

  1. Start a round. The winning number (1-100) is drawn on-chain, encrypted - an euint32 that nobody can read.
  2. Seal your guess. Your number is encrypted in your browser and sent as externalEuint32. One guess per player per round.
  3. Win / lose is computed encrypted. The contract runs FHE.eq(guess, secret) - it never sees a plaintext guess or secret.
  4. Reveal. After the deadline, revealRound() makes the secret publicly decryptable - and only then can anyone see the winning number and the winners.

Why it only works with FHE

On a normal blockchain every number is public. A hidden-number game would be broken before it starts: players could copy the winning number, and the host could change it after seeing the guesses. Here the secret is fixed at round creation, encrypted, and the comparison happens while everything is still encrypted. The host cannot change it, players cannot copy it, and the chain never learns it.

What stays encrypted: the winning number (euint32), every guess (euint32), every win flag (ebool). What is public: that a round exists, who played, and - only after reveal - the winning number and winners.
